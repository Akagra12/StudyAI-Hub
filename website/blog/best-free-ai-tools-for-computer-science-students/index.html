Best Free AI Tools for Computer Science Students (Zero Budget Guide)

⚡ TL;DR: Zero-Cost Developer Arsenal

By registering on GitHub Education with your student ID, you unlock 100% free access to GitHub Copilot, JetBrains IDEs, and cloud credits. Pair this with Google Colab (free Nvidia T4 GPU) and Google Interview Warmup for a complete $0 professional engineering toolkit.
