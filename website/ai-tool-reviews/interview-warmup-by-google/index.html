Google Interview Warmup Review

The free speech-to-text AI mock interview coach built by Google to help computer science students prepare for technical and behavioral interviews.

Tool NameInterview Warmup (Grow with Google)

CSE Student Use Case

Many B.Tech CSE students excel in coding rounds but struggle during the HR and Managerial rounds when explaining past technical projects or handling situational questions (e.g. "Describe a time you resolved a conflict within your project team"). Google Interview Warmup allows students to speak naturally into their microphone and provides instant insights on job-related terms, most-used words, and talking points.

Pros & Cons for CSE Students

Pros

  • Completely free with zero account creation required
  • Real-time speech-to-text transcription with high accuracy
  • Highlights relevant computer science and software terminology

Cons

  • Does not simulate live conversational interruptions from a human interviewer
  • Questions are standard across sessions rather than fully dynamic
