## Internal Anchor Text Rules
1. Exact match anchors are used when linking to specific tool reviews (e.g. `[GitHub Copilot](/ai-tool-reviews/github-copilot/)`).
2. Topical descriptive anchors are used for editorial guides (e.g. `[Best AI Tools for CSE Projects Guide](/blog/best-ai-tools-for-cse-projects/)`).
3. No generic "click here" or "read more" anchor texts are used anywhere in the body content.
