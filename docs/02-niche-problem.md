# 02 - Niche Problem Statement & Target Audience Definition

## Problem Statement
B.Tech Computer Science Engineering students utilize numerous artificial intelligence tools throughout their 4-year degree for coding assignments, competitive programming, machine learning coursework, semester theory exams, and on-campus placement preparation. However, information regarding these tools is fragmented across disjointed medium blogs, Reddit threads, GitHub repositories, and generic directory portals.

Generic directories fail CSE students in three major ways:
1. **Information Noise:** Over 90% of tools on generic AI directories are targeted at marketing, sales, copywriters, or graphic artists.
2. **Lack of Curriculum Alignment:** Generic sites do not categorize tools by engineering subjects (such as Operating Systems, DBMS, Dynamic Programming, or PyTorch).
3. **Pricing Misinformation:** Many platforms fail to highlight student benefits (such as the GitHub Student Developer Pack, free educational licenses, or free cloud GPU tiers), causing students to unnecessarily pay for subscriptions.

## Target Audience Boundaries
StudyAI Hub enforces a strict target audience boundary:
- **Included Audience:** B.Tech Computer Science Engineering (CSE), Information Technology (IT), and Software Engineering students.
- **Excluded Audiences:** Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Electrical Engineering, MBA / Business students, Medical / Nursing, Graphic Designers, Digital Marketers, General public.
